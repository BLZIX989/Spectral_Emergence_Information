## Overview

Spectral Emergence Information Theory (SEIT) is a proposed unifying framework that asks a different question than traditional approaches to a Theory of Everything.

Rather than asking:

> **What is reality made of?**

SEIT asks:

> **What mathematical structure makes stable reality inevitable?**

The framework proposes that observable physics emerges from the spectral properties of a single primitive object: the graph Laplacian of a universal distinction graph. Geometry, particles, gauge fields, thermodynamics, information, and higher-order organization are treated as successive realizations of a common spectral structure.

At its core, SEIT shifts explanation away from fundamental objects and toward the necessity conditions that allow coherent structures to persist.
